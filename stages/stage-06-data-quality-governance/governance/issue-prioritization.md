# Data Quality Issue Prioritization

## Purpose

Not all data quality issues have the same level of impact.

Issue prioritization helps determine which data quality problems should be addressed first based on their potential impact on the business, data, and risk.

## Prioritization Levels

| Priority | Description |
|---|---|
| High | Issues that may have significant business impact, affect critical data, or create high risk. |
| Medium | Issues that have a noticeable impact but do not represent an immediate high risk. |
| Low | Issues with limited impact that can be addressed as part of routine data quality improvement. |

## Prioritization Factors

The following factors can be considered when assigning a priority:

### Business Impact

How much could the issue affect business processes, decisions, or operations?

### Data Impact

How many records or data elements are affected?

### Risk

Could the issue result in incorrect reporting, decisions, compliance problems, or other risks?

## Project Application

In this project, issue priorities are used as an illustrative governance approach.

The `customers.csv` dataset does not contain business impact information, criticality classifications, or organizational risk assessments. Therefore, real business priorities cannot be confirmed from the dataset alone.

The purpose of this prioritization model is to demonstrate how data quality issues could be assessed and managed in a real Data Governance environment.

## Example

A data quality issue affecting a large number of critical customer records would generally receive a higher priority than a minor issue affecting a small number of non-critical records.

Final priority decisions should be reviewed and confirmed by the appropriate Data Owner or business stakeholders.
